What each stage does
Concept uses installed image models. Shape reconstructs the approved image with local Hunyuan3D. Paint preserves geometry and bakes the reference palette and painted material variation into UV textures. Motion preserves an imported rig or builds an editable biped draft. Inspect every clip before approving polish. The local pipeline never sends your prompts or assets to a paid generation API.
Automatic reconstruction and rigging need visual review, especially fingers, antlers, backs and unusual anatomy. A successful job is a candidate, not proof of production quality.
