# M3: terrain, routes and elevated defenses

Owner: Codex on `feature/99-planets-terrain`, stacked on M2 PR #12.
Status: implemented; automated checks passed; visual/feel review open.
Not merged or deployed.

The four campaign terrain profiles use the same height field for rendering,
picking, movement, route costs and placement. Height alone no longer prevents
walking or building. Dry, stable footprints accept towers at elevation; hot
black stone accepts only Mortars (+15% damage), and cold blue-white stone accepts
only Cryo (+10% slow strength, with a global 70% slow ceiling). Mixed footprints
are rejected. The range guide uses 3D acquisition spheres and Mortar's actual
inner exclusion and sampled terrain intersections; Warden retains a separately
labelled surface leash. Contour endpoints are bisected against the height field.

Movement applies a bounded uphill penalty, swimming at 60% speed with shoreline
hysteresis, and no swimming sprint. Cargo multiplies that movement cost. Ground
orders use directed path costs, while flyers have a separate fixed graph with
a radial ceiling and clearance around extreme mountains. Tower placement never
rebuilds the graph or changes the flyer field. Garrison units can leave their
own footprint. New towers cannot be built around an existing friendly unit.

## Failures retained during development

- A picking shell capped at 6.5 units missed existing peaks and was unsuitable
  for taller terrain. The conservative terrain envelope now owns picking.
- The old 4.5-unit fog shell sliced taller mountains into exposed fragments.
  Campaign fog now follows the terrain mesh. Orbit focus follows terrain height
  and checks camera clearance too.
- Hard continental/canyon cutoffs became several-metre discontinuities when
  amplified. Removing those cutoffs and bounding the canyon gradient estimate
  fixed the sampled shoreline jump. Canyon width now scales with its depth;
  incision is bounded instead of cutting every alpine peak to sea level.
- Frontier spawn remapping could choose disconnected but walkable terrain.
  It now follows the solved route. Spawn scatter is bounded in world units and
  checked for reachability. A stitching margin outside the battlefield wall is
  excluded from movement graphs.
- A second, analytic movement blocker could disagree with a certified route.
  Blocking now uses the route graph, and smoothed corners recover through their
  cell centre. Flyer cells also check local relief, not just their centre.
- The added terrain selector overflowed at 720p. Compact map cards retain Begin.
- The M2 cargo fixture compared different slopes. A level-water control now
  isolates the cargo multiplier; its position setup remains explicitly a fixture.
- Retention was previously described as 10% extra radius. The implementation
  uses 1.1 times squared distance, or about 4.9% extra radius. The HUD is corrected
  without changing the combat tolerance.
- Following focal height during a steep drag made its projection fold over,
  leaving a 55.5-pixel grab error. Dragging now holds focal altitude, then eases
  onto the new ground in 0.45 seconds. The clearance solver re-solves its orbit
  instead of displacing the finished camera, which had caused a zoom dip.

## Remaining review

The extreme alpine fixture took about 42 seconds including boot and fixture
work on this test machine; startup performance is not certified. The alpine
view can be heavily occluded by nearby cliffs even when the eye has clearance.
Player camera feel, cliff framing, contour smoothness and a sustained live
frame-time review remain open. M4 owns the broader foliage/model/animation
sweep. These screenshots are evidence for review, not an art approval.
