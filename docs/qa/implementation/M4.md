# M4: weapons, loot and strike choreography

Owner: Codex on `feature/99-planets-weapons`, stacked on M3 PR #13.
Status: implemented; automated mechanics checks passed; visual review open.
Extraction and checkpoint retention follow in M5. Not merged or deployed.

Four weapon families now reach live combat: sword sectors, narrow spears,
travelling carbines with swept collision, and arcing explosive lobbers. Two
equipment slots and twelve backpack spaces support comparison, compatibility,
three modular part slots, salvage and explicit replacement when full. Native
commander techniques and a basic sword remain available. Equipment changes
during attacks commit after recovery; an in-flight projectile keeps its launch
stats. The solo inventory pauses play and suspends possession input.

Ordinary enemies have a 2% weapon chance, Aegis 10%, and Colossus guarantees one.
Loot uses a separate seeded RNG and persistent item identity, not pooled enemy
identity. Ground items remain visible until collected or deliberately salvaged.
Drops on disconnected ground fall back to the heart's reachable node. Item
tiers are bounded, with ancient, technological and empowered presentation.
Core colors, long heads, handling traits and part tradeoffs distinguish builds.
This is a four-family prototype, not a production-size authored weapon library.

Shared strike phases govern anticipation, release and recovery. Enemy windups
lock a red 3D sector whose definition also decides damage; leaving it before
release avoids the blow. Frost and burn reach live combat and reset on pooled
reuse. Weapon-equipped AI uses its weapon's real attack. Existing native AI
retains its previous melee policy. Tower aiming eases near alignment, and gait
distance follows actual surface travel rather than nominal flat speed.

Foliage wind now deforms depth shadows and inverse-transpose normals with the
same uniforms as visible geometry; reduced motion disables sway. Diagnostics
found no reversed faces in the sampled pine/broadleaf meshes. The owner's
original polygon complaint was not reproduced identically, so this correction
does not close every reported foliage artifact.
