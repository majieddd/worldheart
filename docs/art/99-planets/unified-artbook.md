## Scenario and campaign

Protect and claim a planet, venture to the next, return home to store loot or
make the claimed planet home. Random planets are meaningful because terrain,
native inhabitants and the current conflict interact. Mixed factions require a
scenario, such as anomalies invading an Axiom world. The invasion is semi-serious;
the Roblox cast adds Seriously Funny Vibes.

Information First, Modeled by Decoration means role and relevant stats stay clear,
while maker identity, construction and character explain why the profile exists.
Surfaces carry the illustration. Efficient development uses browser staging today,
ports confirmed checkpoints to Roblox and can move the primary loop to Roblox
when that is easier to build and validate. Reuse checks instead of multiplying
launchers; save usage by avoiding redundant work, not by reducing quality.

## Xeno canon and proposal boundaries

- Physical: reddish brown, strong, enduring, slow, few special abilities. Lives
  on Mars and inhabits meteors for space travel.
- Void: purple, physically weak, strong elemental resistance, teleportation scouts.
  First invaders on Earth. Element resistance is not immunity.
- Frost, Fire and Poison: blue, orange/red and green. Proposed controller, burst
  and denial profiles are illustrative targets, not implemented balance.
- The intercepted meteor reveals brown inhabitants; the first ground arrival is
  purple scouts through portals. NASA's real safe Apophis flyby remains distinct
  from this invented interception and invasion.
- The glyph system is an original 26-letter substitution alphabet with Latin keys
  and invented Adamic-inspired readings. Preserve the original A/Y/L/M/O paths.
  It is not a verified ancient script or a complete spoken language.
