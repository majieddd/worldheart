01 / SHARED CORNERS

Edges that agree

An irregular quad grid shares corner heights. Six transition families form soft corners and flat shelves. Both sides use the same tessellation, so this compares shaping rather than extra polygons.

02 / PLAYABLE SPACE

Routes before dressing

The kit reserves ten-metre corridors along low-cost routes in the source terrain. A separate check measures slope and body clearance on the actual rendered triangles. Compatible tiles alone cannot guarantee a path.

03 / LARGER PATTERNS

An overlook that fits

Where several cells fit, one raised lookout and its ramp face a route. This tests the final section of the video: a landmark spanning multiple pieces. The fit is skipped when there is insufficient room.
