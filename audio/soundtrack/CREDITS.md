# Owner soundtrack

These nine tracks were supplied by the owner and explicitly authorized for the
public game in this task. They retain their original MP3 bytes, stereo content
and metadata. The manifest records exact hashes and measured loudness/true peaks.
Playback uses attenuation only, with no lossy re-encoding or noise-floor boost.
This is owner-provided music, not part of the CC0 effects license.

Title Song plays at the game title screen after a gesture. Lobby Song 1 and 2
alternate in the preparation lobby. Planet 1 is the default exploration track;
the playful track alternates on later even-numbered planets, and Tropical World
serves garden/jungle/ocean/Earth themes. Boss Fight 1 accompanies ordinary bosses;
Desert Boss Fight handles arid themes, and Cinematic Trailer/Boss Fight handles
late boss waves. There is no trailer screen yet. Boss selection takes priority
over exploration. At most two tracks stream during a short crossfade.

The old procedural wind/pad is disabled. Music has its own volume/enable controls
and follows game mute. Hidden tabs pause media. No track downloads before the
first user gesture; the comparison page offers lazy native audio players.

User-supplied audio is preserved as authorized. No classifier or metadata is
being presented as a listening certification of its content.
