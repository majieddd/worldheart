# Battle arrangements for listening approval

Five 96-second instrumental-only briefs for Garden, Canopy, Dune, Frozen and Molten planets.
Generated locally with ACE-Step 1.5 turbo using owner-supplied soundtrack passages.
This route is reference-conditioned cover/arrangement generation. It can retain melodic
material from the references; these are not claimed to be unrelated compositions.

The owner authorized the nine original source songs for the public game. Those files
and the five owner-approved calm arrangements were preserved byte-for-byte.

Model: ACE-Step 1.5, MIT model card. Source model revision
694a9723ff772285c73f0700caacf944d3f02f8d. Local reference subset SHA256:
bca0bfde54bc7177dc5939ca9e4fc94314b919f084044cd647347cdeac9bbd47.
ComfyUI revision 36da3ff763687eab86a35e1019995dd1fb369b0d. The unused audio-code
planner is absent; full-length source audio conditions the diffusion process.

Every final graph attenuates decoder float output by 9 dB before lossless export.
Mastering uses static level matching and short edge fades, without denoising or
additional dynamics compression. Per-track prompts, seeds, references, hashes,
levels and durations are in manifest.json. The Dune first take failed the sustained
content check and was replaced; its files and measurements remain in the QA archive.

No voice model or vocal prompt was requested. The agent cannot listen; prompt text,
waveform checks and metadata do not prove an absence of voice-like sounds or establish
musical quality. These battle files remain approval-only and are never automatically
selected by gameplay. Owner-approved calm pieces play on peaceful home planets.

References: https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5 and
https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI. Source revisions were verified during U148.
